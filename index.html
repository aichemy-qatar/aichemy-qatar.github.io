06 · Questions

What teams ask first.

What can you optimize?

The whole stack between your model and the GPU: serving settings, batching and scheduling, and GPU kernels, for inference and training. The results above are on vLLM, llama.cpp and verl, on H100s and an RTX 3090; tell us what you run.

What access do you need?

Your model, a sample of real prompts, and access to a GPU machine like the ones you serve on. LEVI runs there.

Does our code or data go to an outside AI service?

No. LEVI runs its search with open-weight models on your own GPUs, so your code, prompts and data never leave your network.

How do you make sure nothing breaks?

Every change is checked against your current setup: the same outputs on your prompts, and measured on your hardware. You get the changes as a patch to review, and rolling back means reverting it.
